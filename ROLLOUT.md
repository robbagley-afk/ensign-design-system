## Design-system questions for Rob

- 2026-09-29: `--border-strong` (#94a3b8) is about 2.6:1 on white, under the 3:1 control-boundary rule in the ui-ux-principles gates, yet rule 8 assigns it to controls. Four helper reviews flagged it. Apps now follow rule 8 (controls moved off structural `--border`); changing the token or moving controls to `--input-border` (#64748b) is a design-system decision.
- 2026-09-29: two apps (major-career-explorer, resume-coach-ai) had lines holding only U+00A0 in their CSS, which silently invalidated `:root` aliases. The static audit does not check for it yet.
- 2026-09-29: the runtime audit only sees what is visible on load, so contrast defects in hidden panels (career-fair voice badges, transcription processing button) passed.
